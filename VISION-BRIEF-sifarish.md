## THE DREAM
Sifarish is an all-powerful team of personal agents that gets me interned at a dream
place with good pay. That's all I want and need from it.

It should move the way real teams of professional agents move — each one best at its
craft, working together, nothing dropped. LinkedIn promised me this and doesn't deliver;
it brings me nothing good. I want Sifarish to outperform LinkedIn and Wellfound, both
individually and together. Latest opportunities, from anywhere in the world, so that no
opportunity is ever lost — and none lost because of some mistake at my end.

It should take a job description and produce a top-class resume. Literally top-class. My
resume framing has been weak and the formatting poor; that has to end.

Presenting truth well is the whole craft. It must never invent anything — but it must
present what is true at 100% of its potential. That's what a wise, sharp human does, and
that's the standard.

Using it should feel like talking to Claude: intelligent, understands what I mean,
helpful. I should be able to edit anything, add achievements or anything else whenever I
want, without getting stuck anywhere.

The feeling when it works: mainly **rich and explorable** — everything in front of me,
mine to move through — and **calm and trustworthy** — I know nothing is slipping. Fast
and invisible matters too, but those two lead.

Its own existence is part of the pitch: when someone realises the resume in front of
them was built by the thing I built, that should land.

## SUCCESS, IN HIS WORDS
It gets me selected where my heart wants to be. An internship at a dream place, with good

## THE AUTONOMY CHARTER
He wants to be interrupted only for:
1. Spending real money / exceeding a free tier
2. Deleting or migrating existing data, or anything irreversible — **including anything
   that could get his LinkedIn or Wellfound account restricted or banned. Those accounts
   are load-bearing for the job hunt this project exists to serve; treat their loss as
   irreversible and escalate before doing anything that risks it.**

4. A genuine product-direction fork where both branches are expensive
5. A missing credential — ask once, in one message, with exact baby-step instructions
   for where to get it

Everything else — library choices, naming, copy, missing assets, design judgment,
ambiguity, scope questions, what to do when something fails twice — decide it, log it in
DECISIONS.md, keep moving. He is not available for back-and-forth; he is trusting the
team's expertise, exactly like a founder trusts a real team.
